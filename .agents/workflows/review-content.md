# /review-content <path>
Check and report as a table (item / pass / fix):
- Accuracy of facts, formulas, units, pin numbers (flag anything unverified).
- Safety compliance (`.agents/rules/safety.md`).
- Pedagogy: objectives met, intuition before formula, one idea at a time, worked example, quiz quality (plausible distractors, explanations).
- Reading level and tone for ages 12–18; inclusive language.
- Accessibility: alt text, diagram descriptions, colour independence.
- Originality and licensing of text/images.
- i18n readiness (no baked-in text in images).
- Schema/CI pass. Do not approve; hand the report to the maintainer.
