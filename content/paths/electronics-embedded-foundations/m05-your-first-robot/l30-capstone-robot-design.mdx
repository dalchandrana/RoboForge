# Capstone Project: Design Your Own Mini-Robot

Congratulations on reaching **Lesson 30**—the culminating milestone of the **Electronics & Embedded Foundations** curriculum! 

You have mastered the physical fundamentals:
1. **Electricity & Circuit Theory:** Voltage, current, Ohm's law, and Kirchhoff's laws.
2. **Components & Electronics:** Resistors, LEDs, capacitors, transistors, and diodes.
3. **Embedded Microcontrollers:** Arduino architecture, digital I/O, ADC analog reading, PWM timers, and UART serial debugging.
4. **Motion & Power Systems:** DC gearmotors, H-bridges, servo positioners, power budgets, and optical encoders.
5. **Mobile Robotics & Autonomy:** Differential-drive kinematics, optical line following, and ultrasonic sonar navigation.

Now, you will apply the **complete professional robotics engineering cycle** to design, simulate, build, and document your own robot!

---

## 1. The 4-Stage Robotics Engineering Cycle

```
[ 1. PLAN ] ------> [ 2. SIMULATE ] ------> [ 3. BUILD ] ------> [ 4. REFLECT ]
  Goal & BOM           Virtual Twin           Physical Hardware     Troubleshooting
  Power Budget         Code Verification      Wiring & Assembly     Maker Log & Share
```

### Stage 1: Plan & Specify
- **Mission Goal:** What specific task does your robot perform? (e.g. maze navigator, line follower with obstacle stop, remote teleoperated rover).
- **Bill of Materials (BOM):** Choose standard parts from your Starter or Builder Kit.
- **Power Budget:** Calculate peak stall current:
  $$I_{\text{total}} = 2 \times I_{\text{motor\_stall}} + I_{\text{arduino}} + I_{\text{sensors}} + I_{\text{servo}}$$
  Ensure your $4\times\text{AA}$ battery pack can supply this current without voltage sag below $4.5\,\text{V}$.
- **Pinout Allocation Table:** Map every sensor and driver pin to Arduino I/O, avoiding pin conflicts.

### Stage 2: Simulate in RoboForge First!
Before wiring a single physical jumper wire:
1. Verify component resistor values and driver wiring in the **⚡ Circuit Simulator**.
2. Test sketch logic and serial outputs in the **🤖 Arduino Simulator**.
3. Validate steering thresholds and state machines in the **🏎️ 2D Robot Simulator**.

### Stage 3: Assemble Physical Hardware
- Mount motors, wheels, and caster ball to the chassis plate.
- Install the L298N motor driver and Arduino Uno securely with standoff screws.
- Connect power lines, verifying polarity (Red $= +$, Black $= \text{GND}$).
- Double-check **common ground**: the battery negative, L298N ground, and Arduino GND must all be tied together!

### Stage 4: Test, Calibrate & Reflect
- Test subsystems individually:
  1. Power LED on?
  2. Motor test: run each wheel forward and reverse for 1 second.
  3. Sensor test: print sensor values to the Serial Monitor.
  4. Autonomous loop: place in floor arena and tune control constants ($K_p$ or distance thresholds).
- Record build notes and photos in your **Workshop Build Log**!

## 2. Capstone Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | Standard | Evidence Required |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Engineering Plan** | Clear mission statement, complete BOM, power budget calculation, and pinout table. | Written design notes or schematic. |
| **2. Virtual Simulation** | Circuit netlist verified, sketch compiled, and autonomous navigation tested in 2D arena. | Successful virtual test run in RoboForge. |
| **3. Hardware Build** | Neatly wired physical robot conforming to Class III SELV safety directives ($\le 12\,\text{V}$, $\le 2\,\text{A}$). | Physical photos or workshop assembly notes. |
| **4. Reflection & Learning** | Documented troubleshooting observations: what failed first, how it was solved, and future improvements. | Completed entry in Workshop Build Log. |

---

<Callout type="safety">
**Capstone Safety Checklist:**
1. **Low-voltage Class III SELV:** All robot electronics must operate at $\le 12\,\text{V}$ DC and $\le 2\,\text{A}$. Battery packs must be AA or 9V alkaline/NiMH. Never use raw unmanaged LiPo pouch cells or mains power.
2. **Current-limiting protection:** Ensure an inline slide switch or fuse disconnects the battery pack when the robot is unattended.
3. **Inspect before powering on:** Perform a manual continuity and short-circuit check between $+V_{\text{bat}}$ and $\text{GND}$ using a multimeter in resistance mode before inserting batteries!
</Callout>
